## Hypothesis & working thesis
**Hypothesis:** A reviewer of an early-career professional portfolio may value immediately verifiable education, specific duties and transferable competencies. This is not verified audience research.

**Working thesis:** _Aurelya's professional story can be made distinctive through the relationship between procedural legal administration and disciplined, public-facing operational work — without pretending she is an experienced attorney or a project owner._

### Why these aren't just seven Atlas clones
The source is a legal-administration and operational CV, not the fictional architecture brief used earlier in the Atlas preview. The seven are differentiated by **the organization of evidence**, from dossier to task process to time to index to letter to file navigation to photographic introduction. We reused the *idea of state-synchronized content* from source engineering studies, not their compositions, palettes or source assets.

## Concept recommendation (provisional)
For a law-office/administrative recruitment context, **01 The Record** is the strongest initial hypothesis: its information order makes source credibility visible. **02 Work / Method** is a close alternative when the client wants to emphasize transferable operational skills. **05 The Letter** is a low-friction option if the real audience prefers concise, calm reading over an interface.

No final client-facing design should be chosen without verifying the preferred role, audience and consent. The preview intentionally includes all seven directions for comparative testing.

## Source/asset rights and publication
The public repo does not contain the raw CV, contact phone, full-resolution photos, original photo ZIP, or claims of permission from the photographed person. The demo uses two compact photographic derivatives from the uploaded archive. Professional email is present as a contact action because it was listed on the provided CV; the subject should confirm preferred public contact before publishing an official site.

## Preliminary anti-generic challenge
- **Identity swap:** unlike generic "creative professional" pages, the concepts foreground the precise court internship, named operations roles and degree. A visual-only screenshot could still be repurposed for other users, so this is only partly addressed.
- **Skeleton test:** dossier tabs ≠ task verb matrix ≠ timeline ≠ index accordion ≠ editorial letter ≠ file inspector ≠ cinematic portrait. They are different enough to test layout choices, though all remain experimental.
- **Rationale test:** every lead pattern is connected to a CV fact and the hypothesized reader's need.
- **Blind perception:** NOT YET TESTED. No external reviewers or subject feedback.

**Decision:** keep all seven live as an experiment. No artificial design-quality scores and no declaration that one is objectively best.
